# Executive Summary – Customer Churn Analysis

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand customer churn and identify customer characteristics associated with higher churn. The analysis can help the business identify areas where customer retention efforts can be focused.

## Key Findings

### 1. Customer Churn

The analysis measures the overall percentage of customers who have left the service. The churn rate provides a high-level view of customer retention performance.

### 2. Contract Type

Contract type shows differences in churn behaviour. Customers with shorter-term contracts can be analysed separately from customers with longer-term contracts to understand retention patterns.

### 3. Customer Tenure

Tenure is an important customer characteristic. Customers with shorter tenure show different churn behaviour compared with customers who have stayed for longer periods.

### 4. Monthly Charges

Monthly charges differ between churned and retained customers. Higher charges can be investigated together with contract type and service characteristics to understand customer behaviour.

### 5. Payment Method

Churn patterns vary across payment methods. Payment method can therefore be included in customer retention analysis.

## Statistical Analysis

Pearson correlation was used to examine the relationship between customer tenure and churn.

Descriptive statistics including mean, median, standard deviation, and distribution analysis were also performed.

## Three Actionable Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 – Focus on New Customers

Create an early-stage customer retention program for customers during their first few months of service.

Possible actions:

- Welcome offers
- Early customer support
- Onboarding communication
- Service usage guidance

### Recommendation 2 – Review Contract and Pricing Options

Analyse customers with higher churn rates by contract type and monthly charges.

Possible actions:

- Offer suitable long-term plans
- Provide personalised plan recommendations
- Review pricing for high-risk customer segments

### Recommendation 3 – Improve Payment Experience

Investigate customer groups associated with higher churn by payment method.

Possible actions:

- Encourage convenient automatic payment options
- Send payment reminders
- Simplify payment processes
- Provide support for payment-related issues

## Conclusion

The analysis provides a data-driven view of customer churn. Customer tenure, contract type, monthly charges, and payment method can be used to segment customers and identify areas for further retention analysis.

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive way for business teams to explore churn patterns across different customer groups.
