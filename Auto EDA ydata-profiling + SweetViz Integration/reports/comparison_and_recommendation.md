# AutoViz vs Sweetviz — Comparison and Recommendation

## Comparison (100 words)
AutoViz and Sweetviz both automate exploratory data analysis, but their reports serve slightly different needs. AutoViz is useful when an analyst wants a broad visual exploration of a dataset and wants to quickly inspect distributions, relationships and potential patterns. Sweetviz provides a polished, structured HTML report with variable summaries and comparisons, making it convenient for reviewing individual columns and communicating findings. Both tools can save time, but neither replaces checking data definitions, validating unusual values or applying business context. For business stakeholders, I would usually recommend Sweetviz because its organized report is easier to navigate and share. Analysts can use AutoViz for complementary exploration.
