# Retail KPI Definitions

This document defines 10 key performance indicators (KPIs) for a retail business.

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency | Target | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | SUM(Revenue) | Sales Manager | Daily | ≥ ₹75,000/month | Lagging |
| Total Orders | COUNT(Order ID) | Sales Manager | Daily | ≥ 20 orders/month | Lagging |
| Average Order Value | Total Revenue / Total Orders | Sales Manager | Weekly | ≥ ₹30,000 | Lagging |
| Total Quantity Sold | SUM(Quantity) | Sales Manager | Daily | ≥ 100 units/month | Lagging |
| Gross Profit | Revenue - Cost | Finance Manager | Monthly | ≥ ₹150,000 | Lagging |
| Gross Margin % | (Revenue - Cost) / Revenue × 100 | Finance Manager | Monthly | ≥ 25% | Lagging |
| Revenue Growth % | (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue × 100 | Business Manager | Monthly | ≥ 10% | Lagging |
| Order Growth % | (Current Orders - Previous Orders) / Previous Orders × 100 | Sales Manager | Monthly | ≥ 10% | Lagging |
| Product Demand | Quantity Sold / Total Quantity × 100 | Product Manager | Weekly | ≥ 5% per key product | Leading |
| Customer Purchase Activity | Number of customer orders | Marketing Manager | Weekly | Increasing trend | Leading |

## Leading vs Lagging Indicators

### Leading Indicators

Leading indicators provide an early indication of future business performance.

- Product Demand
- Customer Purchase Activity

These KPIs can help identify changes in customer and product activity before they affect overall revenue.

### Lagging Indicators

Lagging indicators measure results that have already occurred.

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Quantity Sold
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Revenue Growth %
- Order Growth %

## KPI Ownership

Each KPI has an assigned business owner who is responsible for monitoring the KPI and taking action when performance falls below the target.

## KPI Monitoring Frequency

Daily KPIs are useful for operational monitoring, while weekly and monthly KPIs are useful for management and business performance reviews.
