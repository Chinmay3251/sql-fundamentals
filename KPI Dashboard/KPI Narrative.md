# KPI Narrative

## Retail KPI Performance Summary

The KPI dashboard provides an overview of sales performance using revenue, orders, average order value, quantity sold, gross profit, and gross margin.

### Revenue

Total revenue is the primary measure of retail sales performance. The revenue trend chart helps identify increases and decreases in sales over time. Changes in revenue can be investigated by comparing regions, products, and order dates.

### Orders

Total orders indicate the level of customer purchasing activity. An increase in orders generally indicates higher transaction activity, while a decrease may require further investigation into customer demand or sales activity.

### Average Order Value

Average Order Value measures the average revenue generated per order. Changes in this KPI can indicate whether customers are purchasing higher- or lower-value products.

### Gross Profit

Gross profit is calculated by subtracting cost from revenue. It shows the amount remaining after product costs are considered.

### Gross Margin

Gross margin measures profitability as a percentage of revenue. A decline in gross margin may occur when costs increase or when a larger proportion of sales comes from lower-margin products.

### Regional Performance

The regional revenue chart helps identify differences in sales performance between regions. Regions with lower revenue can be investigated further to understand product demand and customer purchasing patterns.

### Product Performance

Product-level analysis helps identify products contributing significantly to revenue and products with lower sales activity.

### Leading Indicators

Product demand and customer purchase activity are leading indicators because changes in these metrics may provide an early signal of future sales performance.

### Lagging Indicators

Revenue, orders, average order value, gross profit, and gross margin are lagging indicators because they measure business results that have already occurred.

## Anomalies

Any significant increase or decrease in revenue, orders, or gross margin should be investigated by comparing the affected date, region, and product.

The dashboard filters can be used to identify the source of unusual changes and determine whether the change is related to a specific product, region, or period.

## Conclusion

The KPI dashboard provides a single-page view of retail performance and helps management monitor sales, profitability, customer activity, regional performance, and product performance.
